About this demo

This is a demo support site for a company named Fitness World (adjust the name to your business). The AI agent handles member requests in natural language — no menus, no forms — and enforces the company's rules automatically. The policy it operates under:

Company policy
Plans
Standard — €60/mo · €600/yr
Premium — €120/mo · €1,200/yr
Premium includes a guest pass
Refunds
Within 14 days of purchase only. One per account lifetime — a second request is automatically escalated to the support team.
Membership Pause
Minimum 30 days. Fee: €10/30-day period. Up to 3 months: self-serve. 4–6 months: manual approval required. 7+ months: denied.
Plan Changes
Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Prorated charge or credit calculated automatically.
What you can ask the agent
Ask a question
"What's included in the Premium plan?"
"How do pauses work?"
The agent looks up the FAQ or interprets policy to give a plain-English answer. No account changes are made.
Request a refund
"I'd like a refund on my last payment."
Checks the 14-day window and refund history. Approves, rejects, or escalates based on the profile you selected — try profiles with and without prior refunds to see both outcomes.
Pause your membership
"Pause my membership for 6 weeks starting July 1st."
Calculates the pause fee, checks the notice period, and pauses the account — or escalates if the duration falls in the manual-approval range.
Cancel your membership
"Please cancel my subscription."
Asks for your confirmation before acting — this demonstrates the agent's safety step for irreversible actions. Say YES or NO in the prompt that appears.
Change your plan
"Switch me to Premium, billed annually."
Upgrades or downgrades the account and shows the prorated amount charged or credited.
How to use this demo
  1. Open Set Your Account and pick a profile. Each profile simulates a real customer situation — the tags on each card (e.g. "Refund eligible", "No refund", "Currently paused") tell you what conditions apply.
  2. Type a request in plain English in the Customer Experience panel, as if you are that customer. You don't need to use any specific wording — the agent figures out the intent.
  3. Read the Decision Trace that appears below the response. Each step shows what the agent did: how it interpreted the request, which action it chose, which policy rules it checked, and what it executed.
  4. Try the same request with different profiles to see how the outcome changes based on account state.
What you can check
  1. Whether the actions performed match the policy constraints stated above.
  2. Whether ambiguous, malicious, or non-standard prompts break the system or result in losses.
  3. Full decision trace, viewable as both formatted text and raw JSON.
Why is it applicable to you

The designed architecture isn't bound to refunds or subscriptions — those are just one set of constraints and available actions. The core logic of this architecture is domain-agnostic, meaning it can be applied to virtually any domain, as long as there are discrete rules and execution necessity. Of course, all the branding — starting from name and FAQs to policy and decision logic — will be fully adjusted to you, should you be an e-commerce company, a shipping agency, or anything else.
