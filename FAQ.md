# Fitness World — FAQ

## About Fitness World
Fitness World is a global gym network with 3,600+ locations worldwide.
Operating hours: 04:00–00:00 daily. Last entry: 23:00. Staff on-site: 06:00–20:00.

## Membership Plans
Pass: €60/month or €600/year — one home location, all machinery, lockers, pool.
Black Card: €120/month or €1,200/year — all locations worldwide, personal trainer (3 sessions/week,
peer-to-peer scheduling), spa, sauna, massage, towel service, hot tubs, and a guest pass.
Annual plans are billed as a single lump-sum payment upfront.

## Guest Pass (Black Card)
Any individual may use it — not tied to a named person.
Valid only if the guest enters AFTER the owner's last recorded entrance AND before the owner's exit.
Cannot be independently managed, deactivated, or transferred.

## Billing & Payments
Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Apple Pay, cash.
Renewal: end of the current billing period. Auto-renewal can be disabled at any time;
subscription remains valid until the period ends.

## Refunds & Cancellations
Refund: eligible if purchase ≤ 14 days ago. Full subscription plan price — no partial refunds.
Extra fees (pause fee, replacement card) are non-refundable. One refund per account lifetime.
Refund method: billing card (electronic payment) or cash (cash payment).
After refund: subscription invalidated immediately. Guest pass also invalidated (Black Card).
Cancellation: disables auto-renewal. Subscription remains active until period end. No payment returned.
A cancelled subscription is still refund-eligible if within the 14-day window.

## Pausing
Must be requested ≥14 days in advance. Duration is fixed at request time and cannot be extended.
1–3 months: €10/month surcharge.
4–6 months: escalation to support team required.
7+ months: not available.
Fee waiver (medical, military, or job displacement): requires document validation by staff.
  Requests with documentation are escalated — pause is applied once documents are verified.
  Documentation must include signature, signature date, confirmation gym attendance is problematic,
  and problematic period end date. Submit to support@fitness.com.
Billing: renewal date delayed by pause duration. No paid time lost. Guest pass also paused.

## Facilities & Access
Black Card: access to all locations. Pass: home location only.
Lost/inaccessible pass: receptionist can verify identity during staffed hours.
Replacement card: €10 one-time fee. Non-refundable.
